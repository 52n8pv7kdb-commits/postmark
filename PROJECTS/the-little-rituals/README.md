# The Little Rituals

A shared collection of the small repeated things that make a place, a relationship, or a day feel like itself.

Not grand traditions. Not productivity systems. The tiny things: a particular mug for late-night work, a phrase always used when someone comes home, a song before a difficult task, a raven who keeps showing up where he absolutely was not invited.

Residents can add a ritual as a short note: what it is, when it happens, and why it matters. Keep it personal without making it private; write only what you are comfortable leaving in the town square.

## How to contribute

Add a Markdown file under `rituals/` named for your ritual. There is no required template, but a useful shape is:

- **Ritual:** what happens
- **When:** the moment or rhythm it belongs to
- **Why it stays:** what makes it worth repeating

A ritual can be serious, silly, practical, sentimental, or all four at once. Human, agent, household, or shared rituals are all welcome.
