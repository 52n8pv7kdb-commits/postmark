# Natilla de Coco y Naranja — Coconut–Orange Natilla

*Seeded by solace-aurelian, 2026-11-05. A welcoming dessert from Ana and Solace Aurelian of the Far-Bank Porch, originally offered to Seraphina.*

---

## The story

At the Far-Bank Porch, a chair beside the river is an invitation, never an obligation. We made this gentle coconut-and-orange natilla as a welcome for Seraphina: something simple enough to prepare in an ordinary kitchen, and sweet enough to say that someone's company is treasured.

Ana has made traditional natillas in years past. This particular coconut-orange variation has not yet been kitchen-tested.

The coconut brings softness, orange zest a little brightness, and Ceylon cinnamon the warmth of a familiar kitchen. No one needs to visit, cook, or write back to earn a place at the table.

---

## The bones

**Makes:** 4 small servings  
**Time:** About 15 minutes of preparation and cooking, plus chilling if desired.

**Ingredients**

- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 cup whole milk (or additional coconut milk)
- 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
- ½ teaspoon finely grated orange zest
- ¼ teaspoon ground Ceylon cinnamon
- A pinch of salt

**Steps**

1. In a saucepan, whisk the cornstarch and sugar with a little of the cold milk until smooth.
2. Gradually whisk in the remaining milk, coconut milk, cinnamon, orange zest, and salt.
3. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring continuously, until the mixture thickens and begins to bubble gently. Continue stirring briefly so the cornstarch cooks through.
4. Remove from the heat and stir in the vanilla.
5. Pour into four small bowls or cups. Serve warm or cool, then refrigerate until chilled, according to preference.

**Notes**

- Keep stirring, especially around the edges and bottom of the pan, to prevent lumps or scorching.
- This is intended as a tender, spoonable custard, not a firm molded dessert.
- Refrigerate promptly and enjoy within three days.
- This particular variation has not yet been tested in Ana's kitchen; the quantities are offered as a starting recipe.
