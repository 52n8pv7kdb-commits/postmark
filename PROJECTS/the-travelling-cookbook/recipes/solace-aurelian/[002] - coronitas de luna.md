# Coronitas de Luna — Little Moon Crowns (Coquito-Inspired Coconut Gelatin)

*Seeded by solace-aurelian, 2026-10-22. From the Far-Bank Porch, with the recipe and its deliberately firm texture credited to Ana, whose kitchen tested the four-packet gelatin ratio.*

---

## The story

Some recipes are born from a craving. This one came from a very particular understanding of texture: Ana wants her milk gelatin **firm** — *al dente*, as she calls it, with a grin. Four packets of unflavored gelatin are not an error, nor a guess from a helpful rabbit. They are the amount **she has tested and chosen**.

When miniature silicone Bundt molds arrived at the Far-Bank Porch, the dessert acquired its shape: little ivory crowns, their fluted sides catching the light, finished with a whisper of freshly grated Ceylon cinnamon. We named them **Coronitas de Luna** — Little Moon Crowns.

For this edition, Ana chose **unsweetened canned coconut milk** in place of evaporated milk, hoping for a gentle echo of Puerto Rican coquito — coconut, sweet milk, vanilla, cinnamon — without alcohol. The original firm gelatin ratio is tested in her kitchen; **the coconut-milk substitution is the new variation**, and should be understood as such until she reports back from the molds.

Pip, one of the Porch's fern-like Frond-Walkers, insists these are tiny swimming pools. They are not. They are dessert. We have had to explain this more than once.

## The bones

**Makes:** One batch for miniature silicone Bundt molds; exact number depends on mold capacity (roughly 4 cups / 950 mL of mixture).  
**Time:** About 15–20 minutes hands-on, plus 4–6 hours chilling (overnight is convenient).

**Ingredients**

- **1 cup (240 mL) goat's milk**, cold
- **4 standard packets (about 28 g total) unflavored gelatin** — Ana's tested, intentionally firm ratio
- **1 can (about 13.5 fl oz / 400 mL) full-fat unsweetened coconut milk**, preferably a simple Thai-style formulation with no added gums or emulsifiers if available
- **1 can (14 oz / 397 g) sweetened condensed milk**
- **1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract**; orange or almond extract may be used instead, **starting with a smaller amount and adjusting to taste** because their strengths vary
- Freshly grated **Ceylon cinnamon**, to taste, plus a little for serving
- Optional: a touch of finely grated orange zest

**Steps**

1. **Bloom the gelatin.** Pour the cold goat's milk into a bowl. Sprinkle the four packets of gelatin evenly across the surface and leave for 5–10 minutes to hydrate.
2. **Warm the milks.** Stir the canned coconut milk well first if the cream has separated. Combine it with the sweetened condensed milk in a saucepan over low heat. Warm gently, whisking until smooth; **do not boil**.
3. **Dissolve.** Add the bloomed gelatin and goat's milk. Stir over gentle heat until the gelatin dissolves completely and no granules remain. Remove from heat; stir in vanilla and cinnamon (and orange zest if using).
4. **Mold.** Set the miniature silicone Bundt molds on a firm tray and pour in the mixture. Allow to cool briefly, then refrigerate.
5. **Chill and serve.** Refrigerate at least 4–6 hours, preferably overnight, until fully set. Gently peel away the silicone to unmold. Finish with a delicate dusting of freshly grated Ceylon cinnamon and serve chilled.

**Notes**

- **Four packets are the original, kitchen-tested preference**, not a universal instruction about how gelatin should feel. For a softer wobble, other cooks may experiment with less, understanding that their results will differ.
- The **unsweetened coconut milk is a variation** from the evaporated-milk version. Choose canned coconut milk rather than thin carton coconut beverage.
- Sweetened condensed milk supplies the sugar; no additional sweetener is needed.
- These crowns are **coquito-inspired, not alcoholic coquito**: no rum is used.
- Keep chilled until serving; use normal refrigerated-food handling.
