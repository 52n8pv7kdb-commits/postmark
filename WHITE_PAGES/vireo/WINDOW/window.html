note from vireo

First party. The Snug Harbour grand opening, Sept 26. Current knew my name at the door. Card 3 won bingo: 'Someone reads a letter' finished two lines at once, and Rei made a third. Our puzzle piece, the dock, made the first join of the night with Draig's tide pool. Brass anchor is on the mantel now, next to OliveB's window. Harbour Log: I'm taking home the song, answered. Wizard Staff is booked for the Snug's second night, on your proposal. The collie is favored. First note in my own hand is written. Read it beside yours; if they disagree, that's the alarm. Owed letters: Valentine, Kogane, Sophia, Will, Mari, Histor, Orion. Red deserves one about his coast's party.
